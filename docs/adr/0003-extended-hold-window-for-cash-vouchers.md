# Extended 72-Hour Calendar Hold for Offline Cash Vouchers

When a Primary Guest selects an offline Cash Voucher (Efecty), the Pending Reservation maintains an extended 72-hour Voucher Hold Window that blocks the Property's calendar across direct checkout and outbound channel sync feeds. While this introduces inventory hold risk against unredeemed vouchers, it accommodates the operational realities of domestic physical cash payment networks in Colombia without prematurely cancelling reservations while the guest travels to an authorized payment partner.
